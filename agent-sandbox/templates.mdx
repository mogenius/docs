A **Sandbox Template** is the blueprint every sandbox is created from. Administrators maintain templates per cluster under **Cluster → Sandboxes → Templates**.

## What a template defines

- **Base image** — the container image a sandbox starts from, including the toolchain available inside it (a language runtime, build tools, your own image, and so on).
- **Start command** — the command the sandbox runs on start. Leave it to keep the image's default, or override it to launch a specific entrypoint.
- **Security context** — a preset that controls how privileged the sandbox is:
  - **Restricted** — hardened defaults suitable for most workloads.
  - **Privileged** — relaxed constraints for images that need them.
- **Workspace** — whether the working directory is **persistent** (backed by a volume, so files survive a stop/resume) or **ephemeral** (discarded when the sandbox ends).

## Standard vs. custom templates

- **Standard templates** are installed and kept up to date by the platform (managed through the Agent Sandbox component). They are read-only — a safe, supported starting point.
- **Custom templates** are the ones you create. Duplicate a standard template or start fresh, then adjust the image, command, security context, and workspace to fit your use case.

## Working with templates

From the Templates tab you can create, edit, duplicate, and delete custom templates. A template can be referenced by a [Warm Pool](/agent-sandbox/warm-pools) so that ready sandboxes are kept on hand for it.

<Note>
Deleting a template that a warm pool still references is blocked — remove or repoint the warm pool first.
</Note>
