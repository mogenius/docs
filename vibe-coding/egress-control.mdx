Every app is **isolated on the network by default**. An app can reach its own database, but any call to an external service is blocked until the specific host is approved for that app. This gives the platform team a review point for everything a citizen-developer app talks to.

Isolation is enforced by the cluster's CNI through per-app NetworkPolicies — which is why a NetworkPolicy-enforcing CNI is a [prerequisite](/vibe-coding/prerequisites). On a cluster without enforcement, the policies wouldn't take effect and this control would be ineffective.

## How a host gets approved

<Steps>
  <Step title="The Vibe Coder requests hosts">
    When an app needs to call an external service, the agent submits a request listing the exact hosts and a reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The platform team is notified">
    The request appears for Platform Admins to review, and goes to your [notification channel](/vibe-coding/prerequisites) if configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An admin approves">
    You review the hosts and the reason, and approve the ones that are acceptable. Approval is **per app** — granting a host for one app doesn't open it for others.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each request is a small review: is this host appropriate for this app and team? Until a host is approved, the app's calls to it are blocked.
