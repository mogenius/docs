Platform Admins get an overview across every app and team on the cluster — enough to keep the platform healthy, secure, and within its limits. A Vibe Coder sees only their own team; you see everything.

## What you can see

- **Apps by team and status** — the full inventory of apps, who owns each, and where it is in its lifecycle.
- **App details** — for any app, its components, settings, and logs. The abstraction a Vibe Coder works above is fully transparent to you underneath.
- **App hygiene** — how current each app is, open vulnerabilities, and pending updates, so you can spot apps that need attention.
- **Resource usage** — usage against per-team limits, so you can plan an increase before a team is blocked.
- **Audit log** — actions across the platform. See [Audit log](/cluster-management/audit-log).

Underneath, a Prometheus and Grafana stack ships with the platform and feeds these views, with alerting on failures such as missed backups. Requests that need your decision — like [egress approvals](/vibe-coding/egress-control) — surface here and in your notification channel.

<Note>
Monitoring is oversight, not operation. Vibe Coders stay self-service; you keep visibility into hygiene, security, and resource use without having to touch each app.
</Note>
