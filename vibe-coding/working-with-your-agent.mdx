Once your agent is [connected](/vibe-coding/connect-your-agent), building an app is a conversation. You describe what you want in plain language; the agent builds and deploys it.

## Create an app

Tell the agent what you want to build — for example:

> "Build me a small internal tool that lets our team submit and vote on ideas. It should be reachable from inside the company and keep the data in a database."

The agent asks whatever it needs — which **team** the app is for, whether it should be **reachable externally**, whether it needs a **database** — then creates the app and ships it.

## Watch it go live

Follow the app in the mogenius UI as it moves through **Code → Build → Start → Live**. When it's live, you get a **domain**. See [Your Apps](/vibe-coding/your-apps).

## Keep iterating

Keep talking to the agent to evolve the app:

> "Add a page that shows the top-voted ideas."

Each change is built and rolled out automatically. A new version only goes live once it's working, so a broken change won't take your app down.

## When something breaks

If a build fails or the app misbehaves, ask the agent — it can read the logs, fix the problem, and ship again.

<Note>
A couple of things live in the mogenius UI rather than the conversation: watching your apps, and [setting secrets](/vibe-coding/secrets). Everything else is the agent.
</Note>

Your agent can also give an app a database, a company login, external access, and preview environments — just ask. See [App capabilities](/vibe-coding/app-capabilities).
