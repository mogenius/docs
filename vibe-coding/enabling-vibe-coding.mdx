Vibe-coding is activated and governed through the same **Platform Config** process that manages the rest of a mogenius cluster. You enable it once, provide the details you [gathered](/vibe-coding/prerequisites), and the platform provisions everything teams need.

<Note>
This builds on the standard cluster setup. If you haven't installed the operator and configured the platform yet, start with [Installing the mogenius operator](/cluster-management/installing-the-mogenius-operator) and [Platform Config](/cluster-management/platform-config).
</Note>

## Activate the workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable vibe-coding in the Platform Config">
    Turn on the vibe-coding workflow in the cluster's platform configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your services">
    Supply the identity provider, DNS, secret store, backup storage, and optional notification channel from the [prerequisites](/vibe-coding/prerequisites).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The platform sets itself up">
    mogenius installs and configures the vibe-coding stack on the cluster. See [Platform components](/vibe-coding/platform-components).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teams appear from your IdP">
    Groups from your identity provider become teams, and their members can start shipping apps. See [Teams & roles](/vibe-coding/teams-and-roles).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once it's live, Vibe Coders [connect an agent](/vibe-coding/connect-your-agent) and create apps, while you keep oversight from the [admin views](/vibe-coding/monitoring).
